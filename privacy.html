Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how BGM Uploader, the internal tool operated by Ember & Echo ("we"), handles data. BGM Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the tool

BGM Uploader is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Ember & Echo (@ember_echo-music) to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

  • Our own channel (via OAuth, authorized by the channel owner): uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing schedules, and reading our channel's analytics reports.
  • Public YouTube data (via an API key): public metadata of videos and channels (titles, view counts, publish dates) used for content-trend research.

We do not access, collect or store personal information about YouTube viewers or any other users.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and API data are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Public metadata and our own analytics are kept for up to 30 days for planning purposes and then refreshed or deleted. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Data deletion

Data obtained from the YouTube API Services that has not been refreshed is automatically deleted after 30 days. The channel owner can delete all stored data at any time by revoking access (below) and removing the tool's local database. Upon revocation, stored authorization tokens are deleted and no further data is accessed.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

Access granted to BGM Uploader can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.

Contact

For questions, use the contact email listed on our YouTube channel's About page: youtube.com/@ember_echo-music/about.
